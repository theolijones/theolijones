Bet Tips — Getting Started

How to install the app on your iPhone and submit your first tip video · about 5 minutes

Welcome to the Bet Tips team! Bet Tips isn't in the App Store yet, so we install it directly onto your iPhone. The Bet Tips team will send you two links and a sign-up code — just follow the steps below in order.

Before you start, you'll need
  • An iPhone (iOS 15 or newer)
  • Your Sportsbet username
  • A registration link, an install link, and a sign-up code — all sent to you by the Bet Tips team
1

Register your iPhone

Open the registration link we send you on your iPhone (not a computer).

  • Follow the prompts. iOS will download a small profile.
  • Open Settings — you'll see “Profile Downloaded” near the top. Tap it, then tap Install.

This lets us add the app to your specific phone. Tell us once it's done so we can prepare your install.

2

Install the app

Once your phone is registered, we'll send your install link.

  • Open it on your iPhone in Safari and tap Install. The Bet Tips icon appears on your home screen.
If it says “Untrusted Developer” when you first open the app: go to Settings → General → VPN & Device Management, tap the developer name, then tap Trust.
3

Create your account

Open Bet Tips and sign up:

  • Enter your Sportsbet username.
  • Enter the sign-up code we sent you (it works once).
  • Tap Sign up.
4

Record and submit a tip

  • Tap record and film your tip. Add any overlays or backgrounds you like.
  • On the Tip details screen, enter the Bet ID and choose your template.
  • Tap Submit for review — that's it. The team reviews it and it gets processed automatically.
Your templates are set up for you by the Bet Tips team. If your template list is empty, let us know and we'll add one.
Need a hand? Reply to the message that sent you this guide and the Bet Tips team will help you out.
